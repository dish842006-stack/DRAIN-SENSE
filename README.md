# DRAIN-SENSE

**Smart Urban Drainage Health Monitoring and Flood Early-Warning System**

**Application Unique ID:** VK26-IITH-SC-0073  
**Competition:** Vishwakarma Awards 2026–27 — Stage 2  
**Domain:** Sustainable Cities

## 1. Project Overview

DRAIN-SENSE is a proposed low-cost hardware prototype designed to monitor urban drainage conditions and provide an early warning when water levels rise toward a potentially unsafe threshold.

The project aims to support sustainable urban drainage management by helping identify potential blockage or overflow conditions before they develop into more serious problems.

## 2. Problem Statement

Urban drainage systems can experience blockages and excessive water accumulation during heavy rainfall. Delayed identification of these conditions can contribute to localised waterlogging and disruption.

DRAIN-SENSE proposes a simple monitoring and alert system that can be developed and tested as a tabletop demonstrator.

## 3. Proposed Solution

The proposed prototype will use water-level sensing and a microcontroller-based circuit to trigger local visual and audible alerts when configured thresholds are reached.

The initial demonstrator is intended for controlled testing. It is not yet a validated field-deployment system.

## 4. Main Objectives

- Develop a low-cost drainage monitoring demonstrator.
- Detect predefined water-level thresholds.
- Provide local LED and buzzer alerts.
- Test the system under controlled conditions.
- Document calibration, repeatability, limitations, and future improvements.

## 6. Current Project Status

**Status: Concept design and planning.**

Fabrication, calibration, and physical performance testing have not yet been completed. No measured performance results or field-validation claims are being reported at this stage.

The system architecture, circuit documentation, test evidence, and genuine prototype photographs will be added as the work progresses.

## 7. Planned Validation

Testing is planned to assess:

- Water-level detection at selected thresholds.
- Alert activation and reset behaviour.
- Repeatability over repeated trials.
- Response time under controlled conditions.
- Limitations and potential false alerts.

Results will be recorded only after the corresponding tests have been conducted.

## 8. Scope and Limitations

The initial scope is a low-cost tabletop demonstrator. A custom PCB, cloud dashboard, and field deployment are not included in the initial prototype scope.

The prototype is not intended to replace professional drainage inspections or municipal flood-warning systems.
